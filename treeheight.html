How it works
1. Stand well back from the tree (1–1.5× its height)
2. Hold phone at eye level and sight along its top edge like a gunsight
3. Tilt until the top edge points at the trunk base → lock
4. Tilt up until it points at the crown top → lock
5. Get your height estimate instantly
